---
name: whats-waiting
description: Say what is waiting on the person in Hamloy, the requests only they can do and the questions only they can decide, and what each one holds up. Use when they ask "what's waiting on me", "what do you need from me", "anything for me in Hamloy" or similar.
---

1. Call list with kind request, then with kind question. When Hamloy reaches several projects, do it for each project the person is on.
2. Put first what frees the most work: an item's reply says which tasks wait on it.
3. For each request, give its steps as written. For each question, give its options and the one recommended.
4. When the person answers a question, record it with answer, in their words. When they say a request is done, tick it.
5. Say which tasks are free again now.
