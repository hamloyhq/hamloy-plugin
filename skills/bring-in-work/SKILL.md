---
name: bring-in-work
description: Bring a project's open work into Hamloy from wherever it was kept, such as a TODO file, a plan, a backlog or issues. Use when the person asks to move their work into Hamloy, or when Hamloy's project is empty and the folder keeps its work somewhere else.
---

1. Look for where the work is kept: TODO.md, todo.txt, a plan or roadmap, a backlog, notes on what is waiting on the person.
2. Tell the person what you found and ask before bringing it in.
3. Write each list as a Markdown checklist: tasks, requests (what only the person can do) and questions (what they decide, with options), keeping headings, subtasks and what is already done. Call import once with all of it.
4. Leave the old files where they are. Ask whether to delete them, or to leave one line in each saying the work moved to Hamloy.
